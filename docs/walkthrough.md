# Walkthrough script

A captioned screen recording of about two and a quarter minutes at 1920 × 1080, with no voice. It is recorded
from the verified private hosted candidate with fictional leads entered through the signed-in
interface, using only vehicles and ZIP Codes that have saved responses (the intake form's own
defaults, 2020 Subaru Outback in 22150, among them). The captions below are the on-screen text.

The finished MP4 goes privately to the owner first. Where, or whether, it is published is the
owner's separate decision; until then it stays out of the repository. Stills and captions are
checked for account, project, address-bar and identity values before anything is shared, and the
browser's address bar and account menu stay out of frame.

Times are targets. The real times are measured from the finished video and written back here.

| Time | Screen | Caption |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:08 | Title card | LeadFlow AI turns a fictional insurance inquiry into a scored, checked lead and an email draft a person can approve. |
| 0:08–0:18 | Cover page, signed in | This copy runs privately in Google Cloud for one person. A Google sign-in comes before any page loads. |

| 2:10–2:18 | End card | Private demo, fictional data, removed after seven days. Code and evidence: github.com/prentopoulos/leadflow-ai |

## Preview pass

that no caption covers the element it describes, and that no frame shows an email address other
than the fictional `@example.com` lead, a project name, a service URL or the account picture.
